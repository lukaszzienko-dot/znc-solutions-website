Wall panel rental FAQ

What size are the wall panels?

Each panel is 4 feet wide by 7 feet tall. A 20-foot wall uses 5 panels.

Where do you deliver wall panels?

We deliver wall panels across New York City, New Jersey and Connecticut, including Chelsea, Manhattan, Brooklyn, Hoboken and Jersey City.

Can the panels be modular or stationary?

Both. Panels connect into a continuous modular run or stand as freestanding stationary walls. Tell us your run length and layout.

Do you install the panels at the venue?

Ask us about installation when you book. Share your venue, load-in window and layout in the request form and we will tell you what we can arrange.

How do I book wall panels?

Use the booking form on this page with your event date, number of panels and venue, call 917-536-1245, or email info@zncsolutions.com. We confirm availability before anything is reserved.
