Planning portable gallery walls

Count the works you want to hang and how much space each one needs, then add up the wall length. Each panel gives you 4′ of width, so 5 panels make a 20-ft hanging wall. Panels can run in a straight line, turn corners to make bays, or stand back to back for double-sided display. Pair the walls with lounge furniture for an opening night. FAQ

What size are the portable gallery wall panels?

Every panel is a standard 4' wide x 7' tall.

Do you rent art display panels in NYC?

Yes. We deliver art display panels across New York City, New Jersey and Connecticut.

Are these pressurized apartment walls?

No. These are rigid, freestanding 4′ × 7′ event walls and temporary exhibition walls — solid panel walls, not pressurized apartment walls.

Can the panels be used on both sides?

Yes. The panels are double-sided, and they can also be arranged back to back so there is hanging space on both sides of a run. Share your layout with the quote request and we will confirm the panel count.
