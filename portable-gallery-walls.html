Planning portable gallery walls

Count the works you want to hang and how much space each one needs, then add up the wall length. Each panel gives you 4′ of width, so 5 panels make a 20-ft hanging wall. Panels can run in a straight line, turn corners to make bays, or stand back to back for double-sided display. Pair the walls with lounge furniture for an opening night.
