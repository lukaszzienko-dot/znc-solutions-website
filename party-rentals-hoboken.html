Party rentals for Hoboken lofts and rooftops

Hoboken is a dense, walkable Hudson County city, and its apartments, lofts and rooftops tend to call for compact, well-planned setups. ZNC Solutions quotes Hoboken events from Wayne, NJ with furniture and tabletop sized to the floor plan you describe.

Cocktail parties

For a standing party, 24″ round cocktail tables seat 2–3 and 30″ tables seat 3–5, and barstools add perches at the edge of the room. Add an 8′ wooden bar or a custom fabric bar screen, then barware and glassware from the same order. See bars.

Seated dinners and small receptions

For smaller seated groups, a 48″ round seats 6 and a 60″ round seats 8–10. Pair them with Chiavari or folding chairs and linens in colors and textures from the swatch library. See table and chair rentals.

Building access and load-in

Many Hoboken buildings have elevators, stairs or street-parking limits. When you request a quote, tell us the building rules, floor and elevator access so delivery and pickup can be planned around them.
