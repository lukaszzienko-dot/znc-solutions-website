How to plan an exhibition wall layout

Measure the wall you want in feet and divide by 4: each panel adds 4′ of width at 7′ tall, so a 20-ft wall is 5 panels and a 40-ft run is 10. Straight runs suit a single hanging wall, L and U shapes make booth-style bays, and back-to-back runs give you hanging space on both sides. Send your floor plan, show dates and venue with the quote request and we will confirm the panel count. See also portable gallery walls and the main gallery wall panels page.
