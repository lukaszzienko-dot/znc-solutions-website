Sizing a Bergen County order

Start with the guest count and the style of the event. A seated dinner needs a chair for every guest and enough rounds to hold them: a 48″ round seats 6, a 60″ round seats 8–10 and a 72″ round seats 10–12. A standing reception works better with 24″ cocktail tables for 2–3 guests or 30″ tables for 3–5, plus a few barstools and a lounge corner for guests who want to sit. If you are unsure, send the head count and the room size and we suggest a list.
