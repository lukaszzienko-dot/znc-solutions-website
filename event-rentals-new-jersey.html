Planning an event in New Jersey

Tell us the date, venue, guest count and the pieces you want. Include the load-in window and any building access rules, because delivery timing is confirmed in your quote. Weddings, corporate holiday parties, gallery openings, fundraisers and community events can all share one quote list, and a small order is welcome too: many hosts rent only a bar, a lounge grouping or a few wall panels to add to what the venue already has.

Banquet halls, restaurants, country clubs, houses of worship, school gyms, office lobbies, galleries, backyards and private homes each bring their own access questions. A note about stairs, a loading door or a narrow driveway helps us plan the delivery window before the day.
