Choosing chairs by event

Weddings and seated dinners: Chiavari chairs, with the finish chosen to match linens and décor. Ceremonies, talks and screenings: folding chairs set in rows. Rustic or family-style dinners: X-back chairs at farmhouse tables. Cocktail hours: bar stools at high-top tables.

Plan one chair per guest for seated events, and tell us if the ceremony and dinner are in different spaces so we can work out whether chairs move between them or you need two sets.
