Who we are

ZNC Solutions is a party and event rental company based in Wayne, NJ. We rent furniture, tabletop and display pieces for events in New Jersey, New York and Connecticut. There is no public street address; quotes are handled by phone and email.

What we rent

Our catalog includes table and chair rentals, linens, event furniture & lounge rentals, bars, tabletop and glassware, kitchen equipment and 4′ × 7′ gallery wall panels. Browse every item in the rental catalog and build a quote list as you go.

Who we work with

Hosts planning a party, event planners, venues, caterers, galleries and exhibitors can all order from the same inventory. Planners and venues can combine lounge furniture, bars, tables and tabletop into one request.

Where we deliver

We deliver from Wayne across North Jersey, New York City and Connecticut. See Wayne, North Jersey, New York City and the tri-state overview.

How quoting works

Add items to your quote list, then send it with your event date, city and guest count, or call 917-536-1245 or email info@zncsolutions.com. Start on the quote form.
