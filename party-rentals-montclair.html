Party rentals for Montclair events

Montclair is in Essex County; our Wayne, NJ base is in Passaic County. Montclair events usually want tables, chairs and linens that arrive together, so we quote them as one order from the Wayne inventory: seating, table linens, tabletop, bar equipment and optional gallery walls.

Dinners, receptions and open houses

For seated dinners, pair 60″ rounds (8–10 guests) or 72″ rounds (10–12 guests) with Chiavari chairs in the finish that suits your linens. For standing receptions and open houses, 24″ and 30″ cocktail tables with barstools keep guests mingling. Browse table and chair rentals and choose linens from the swatch library.

Backyards, galleries and community spaces

For shows, fundraisers and open studios, our 4′ × 7′ gallery wall panels give you temporary hanging walls. Add event furniture & lounge rentals to build a lounge area around the walls.

Plan the details

When you request a quote, share the guest count, the date, whether the event is indoors or outdoors, and any load-in notes such as stairs or parking. If the party is outside, propane heaters and umbrellas are in the lounge furniture catalog.
