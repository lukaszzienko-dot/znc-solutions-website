Frequently asked questions

How many chairs per round table?

A 48″ round seats 6, a 60″ round seats 8–10 and a 72″ round seats 10–12.

Which chairs pair with cocktail tables?

Barstools, including the White Chiavari Barstool, X Back Barstool and wooden barstools.

What is the difference between the White Chiavari Chair and Barstool?

The White Chiavari Chair is standard height for seated dining. The White Chiavari Barstool is bar-height for high-top and cocktail tables.
