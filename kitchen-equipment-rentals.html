Equipment for caterers and event kitchens

Pop-up kitchens, off-site catering and large parties often need more equipment than the venue has. ZNC Solutions rents kitchen gear from Wayne, NJ for events in New Jersey, New York City and Connecticut, so a caterer can build a working line for one day.

Cook, hold and chill

Cooking equipment includes a full-size electric convection oven, a Cadco oven, countertop and standing propane deep fryers, an induction burner cooktop and a butane stove. For holding, there are chafing dishes, a full-size chafer food pan, a bain marie, a heat lamp, a warming cabinet and Sterno. A single-door refrigerator covers chilling.

Bakery and prep

Proofing cabinets and a half proofing box, baker racks and half baker racks, sheet pans in three sizes and cutting boards are listed for bakery and prep work.

What to tell us

Kitchen needs depend on the menu, the power and fuel available at the venue and the caterer’s plan. Include those details, the delivery address and your load-in window in your quote request. Add tabletop and glassware and serving and display pieces for the front of house.
