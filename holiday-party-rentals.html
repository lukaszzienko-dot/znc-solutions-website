Planning a holiday or New Year's Eve party

Start with the guest count and the format. A cocktail-style party needs a bar, cocktail tables and glassware, with a few lounge pieces for people who want to sit. A seated dinner needs dining tables, chairs, linens and a full place setting from our tabletop and glassware range. For New Year's Eve, plan a flute or coupe for every guest for the midnight toast.

In winter, plan a coat check near the entrance: a coat rack, hangers and ticket numbers. Send your date, venue and guest count as early as you can and we will confirm availability.

FAQ

What can I rent for a holiday party?

Bars and barware, champagne flutes, coupes and wine glasses, cocktail and dining tables, Chiavari chairs and barstools, lounge seating, lanterns and votives, coat racks and hangers, and 4′ × 7′ wall panels.

Do you deliver for New Year's Eve parties?

We deliver across New York City, New Jersey and Connecticut. Send your date and venue as early as you can and we will confirm availability.

Can I rent what I need for a coat check?

Yes. Coat racks, hangers and coat check ticket numbers are all in the catalog. Tell us your guest count and we will suggest how many racks to plan for.
