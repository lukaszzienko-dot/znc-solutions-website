Exhibition walls for Manhattan venues

Manhattan galleries, museums and event spaces use temporary walls to add hanging space for a single show. ZNC Solutions rents 4′ × 7′ panels from Wayne, NJ and quotes delivery to Chelsea, Midtown, downtown and other Manhattan neighborhoods.

Modular runs or stationary walls

Modular panels link into a long continuous wall; stationary walls stand on their own as a freestanding layout. Because each panel is 4 feet wide, a 20-foot wall is five panels and a 16-foot wall is four. Tell us the run length, the layout and the load-in date and we confirm the count.

Load-in in Manhattan buildings

Many Manhattan buildings have freight elevator hours, loading-dock windows and insurance requirements. Include them in your quote request, along with the floor and door widths, so delivery and pickup can be scheduled around them.
