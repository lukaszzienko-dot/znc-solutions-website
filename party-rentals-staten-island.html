Party rentals for Staten Island homes, halls and the waterfront

Staten Island events often look more like a house party or a hall reception than a high-rise loft opening. Families host backyard dinners, milestone parties and holiday gatherings at private homes from Todt Hill to the South Shore. Churches, community centers and clubhouses run receptions and luncheons. St. George and the North Shore add waterfront rooms, and Snug Harbor hosts cultural programs that need seating and, at times, temporary walls. Guests may arrive on the Staten Island Ferry; the rentals themselves travel by truck over the Verrazzano-Narrows, Goethals or Bayonne Bridge. ZNC Solutions delivers the same catalog used in the other boroughs.

Tables and chairs for backyards, dining rooms and halls

A backyard or a finished basement usually takes 48″ or 60″ rounds, or rectangular tables if the host wants one long family-style board. Dining rooms in private homes may only have space for a single 48″ round plus a cocktail table in the foyer. Halls and clubhouses can take a full set of 60″ or 72″ rounds. Chiavari chairs suit a seated celebration; folding chairs are a practical choice when the same set of seats must move from a ceremony on the lawn to a dinner under a house tent the host already has. See table and chair rentals, chair rental, all tables and chairs and barstools. Cocktail tables work on a deck or at a hall entrance during arrivals.

Bars for hall receptions and home celebrations

A church hall or clubhouse may have a pass-through window but no front-facing bar; a home celebration often has only a kitchen island. A rented bar from bar rental or bars gives a clear service point in a foyer, on a covered patio or at the side of a hall, and it keeps drink service out of a working kitchen. If the party is outdoors, tell us about the surface — deck, pavers or grass — and whether the bar will sit under cover.

Lounge furniture for living rooms, decks and foyers

Home parties sometimes need a second seating area because the dining table cannot hold every guest at once. A sofa, chairs and a coffee table from event furniture and lounge rentals or lounge furniture can go in a living room, on a wide deck, or in a hall foyer so older relatives have a comfortable place to sit through speeches. In a clubhouse, the same pieces fill a room that otherwise has only banquet chairs.

Linens for hall luncheons and backyard tables

Outdoor tabletops need cloths that stay put in island breeze and hide rental-table edges; hall luncheons usually want a single color across every round. Linen rentals cover both, and we quote them with the tables. If you already have napkins or a runner, send a photo and we will match the cloths as closely as the library allows.

Gallery walls for cultural rooms and school exhibits

Snug Harbor programs, school or community exhibits and temporary displays in hall lobbies often need hanging surface that is not already on the walls. The panels we rent are double-sided, four feet wide and seven feet tall, so a short wall can show work on one face and a schedule or title on the other. See gallery wall panels and portable gallery walls. Five panels build a 20-foot run, which fits many lobby or classroom-adjacent exhibits.

Bridges, hills, driveways, ferries and parks

Deliveries to Staten Island come by truck over the bridges; the ferry is a passenger service and is not how furniture arrives. Driveways and parking lots are more common here than in Manhattan, which often makes unloading simpler, but hillside streets, tight courts and gated communities still need a plan. Share gate codes, the steepness of a driveway, and whether the party is in a backyard that can be reached only through the house. Waterfront and park gatherings at Silver Lake, Clove Lakes, Conference House, Great Kills or Midland Beach may add a path, a field or a lot that closes at a set hour — include those notes, and the earliest drop-off the site allows, so we can schedule around them rather than guess.

Party rentals on Staten Island: frequently asked questions

Do you deliver party rentals to Staten Island?

Yes. ZNC Solutions delivers to Staten Island, including St. George, Snug Harbor, Todt Hill and the South Shore, with pickup after the event. Deliveries arrive by truck over the bridges, not on the ferry.

Is a backyard or driveway party easier than a city walk-up?

Often, yes. Many Staten Island homes have a driveway or a side yard, which simplifies unloading. Still tell us about hills, tight streets, gates and whether the party is in the backyard.

What do church and community halls usually need?

Rounds or rectangular tables, chairs and linens, and a bar if the hall does not have one. Send whatever furniture the hall already provides so we do not duplicate it.

Can I rent gallery walls for a Staten Island exhibit?

Yes. Double-sided 4′ × 7′ panels are quoted for cultural spaces, school or community exhibits and temporary displays at halls and waterfront rooms.
