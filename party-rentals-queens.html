Party rentals across Queens neighborhoods

Queens holds several event styles on one map. Long Island City has waterfront lofts, rooftops and new residential towers. Astoria mixes restaurant buyouts, backyard gatherings and community rooms. Flushing is known for banquet halls and cultural celebrations. Jackson Heights, Sunnyside, Woodside, Ridgewood and Forest Hills add family milestones, club luncheons and house parties. ZNC Solutions delivers one catalog to all of those settings: tables, chairs, linens, bars, lounge furniture, tabletop and gallery walls.

Tables and chairs for lofts, halls and backyards

A Flushing or Forest Hills banquet hall often needs a full field of 60″ or 72″ rounds, sometimes in numbers the house inventory cannot cover. A Long Island City loft or rooftop leans toward cocktail tables and a smaller seated section. An Astoria or Ridgewood backyard usually fits 48″ or 60″ rounds, with folding or Chiavari chairs that can move from a patio into the house if the weather turns. See table and chair rentals, chair rental, all tables and chairs and barstools. If the hall already owns some tables, tell us the count and size so the quote fills only the gap.

Bars for halls, rooftops and restaurant buyouts

Banquet halls may already have a service counter; a rooftop in Long Island City or a restaurant buyout in Astoria often does not have a second bar for a cocktail hour. A rented station from bar rental or bars can sit in a lobby, on a terrace or in a side room so the dining floor stays clear. Add glassware when the celebration includes a toast or a full cocktail list. Mention whether the venue limits alcohol service to a particular room, so the bar is planned for that room and not moved on the day.

Lounge furniture for lobbies and family gatherings

Long Island City residential buildings and hotel-style event rooms often have a lobby or pre-function area that looks unfinished until it has somewhere to sit. A compact lounge set from event furniture and lounge rentals or lounge furniture gives elders and early arrivals a place to wait, and it can mark a gift or dessert table without using dining chairs. In a house or backyard party, the same pieces can turn a garage bay or a covered porch into a second seating area.

Linens for banquet halls and at-home celebrations

Halls and cultural celebrations often call for a consistent cloth color across many tables; a backyard or apartment party may only need a few cloths that hide folding-table edges and match a balloon or flower palette. Linen rentals cover both scales, and we quote them with the tables so sizes are not guessed later. If the hall has a house linen, say so and we will work around it.

Wall panels for LIC studios and community exhibits

Artists, cultural groups and pop-up exhibitors in Long Island City and western Queens use temporary walls when a studio, lobby or community room has little hanging surface. Our wall panels measure 4 ft × 7 ft and are double-sided, so a short run can divide a room and show work on both faces. See gallery wall panels and portable gallery walls. If you already know the wall length, a 20-foot run is five panels.

High-rises, banquet halls, yards and park edges

A Long Island City tower is a freight-elevator job, with a booked cab and a service entrance, much like a Manhattan high-rise. A house or backyard in Astoria, Jackson Heights or Ridgewood is the opposite: a driveway, an alley or a curb drop, sometimes through a side gate or a basement entrance. Banquet halls usually have a loading zone or a back door sized for catering, which makes table deliveries straightforward if we have the dock hours. Parks such as Gantry Plaza, Astoria Park and Flushing Meadows-Corona Park can host gatherings where the last stretch is a path or a field; tell us about vehicle gates, path width and whether a dolly can roll from the curb to the site. LaGuardia-area traffic is worth planning around on the calendar, so send the event start time early.
