Plan your wall run

Each panel is 4 feet wide and 7 feet tall. A 20-foot run takes five panels; a 12-foot run takes three. Panels connect into a continuous modular wall or stand as stationary exhibition walls to create bays and sightlines. Send a floor plan or a phone sketch with the run lengths and we quote panel count, delivery and setup.

Openings in one order

Opening nights need more than walls. Add 30″ cocktail tables, a wooden bar, stemless wine glasses, ice tubs and a coat rack with check tickets so the gallery wall rental and the reception arrive on the same truck.

FAQ — Gallery wall rentals Hoboken and Jersey City

What size are the gallery wall panels?

Each Wall Panel System panel is 4 feet wide by 7 feet tall. A 20-foot wall uses five panels.

Do you deliver gallery walls to Hoboken and Jersey City?
