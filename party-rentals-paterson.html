Party rentals for Paterson events

Paterson is the Passaic County seat, in the same county as our home town of Wayne, NJ, so Paterson events are part of our regular North Jersey delivery area. Whether you are planning a reception, a milestone birthday, a community gathering or a corporate event, one order can cover tables, seating, linens, tabletop and bar equipment.

Tables and seating

Round tables come in 48″, 60″ and 72″ sizes, seating 6, 8–10 and 10–12 guests. Add 24″ or 30″ cocktail tables for standing areas, and choose Chiavari chairs in white, gold, black, silver, transparent, natural wood or dark wood, or folding chairs for larger headcounts. See all table and chair rentals or the chairs and barstools catalog.

Tabletop, bars and kitchen gear

Build the table with tabletop and glassware, then add an 8′ wooden bar or a custom fabric bar screen from event bar rental. Caterers working a Paterson event can add catering equipment rental such as chafing dishes and coffee urns, or kitchen equipment such as ovens, proofing cabinets and baker racks.

Gallery walls and display

The same 4′ × 7′ modular panels used in New York galleries are available for exhibitions, community displays and photo walls. A 20-foot run uses five panels, and each panel is double-sided. See wall panel rental for details.

Venues and spaces we deliver to in Paterson

Banquet halls, restaurants, houses of worship, cultural and community centers, school gyms, offices, backyards and private homes. Many Paterson venues sit on busy streets with limited curb space, so tell us where a truck can stop, which entrance to use and whether there are stairs. If the event runs late, include the latest pickup time the venue allows.

Delivery windows and timing

Delivery timing is confirmed in your quote. Send the event date, the earliest drop-off and the latest pickup your venue allows. For weekend events, book early so the pieces you want are reserved; we confirm availability by email before anything is held.

Nearby towns we also deliver to

Paterson planners often compare options across the area. We deliver to Wayne, Montclair, Bergen County, Newark and Essex County and the wider North Jersey area, and into New York City and Connecticut too. Start at event rentals in New Jersey for the full list.

Community and cultural celebrations

Paterson's community groups, congregations and families host christenings, quinceañeras, anniversaries, cultural festivals and holiday dinners that often run large. Folding chairs and 60″ or 72″ rounds handle the seating, linens dress the tables, and chafing dishes and food pans keep a buffet warm through a long evening. For a dessert or gift table, add a cocktail table or two near the entrance. Send the head count and we suggest quantities. If the celebration has a program or a first dance, tell us where the open floor needs to be so the table layout leaves room for it, and whether a lounge corner for older guests would help.
