Party rentals for Paterson events

Paterson is the Passaic County seat and sits in the same county as our home base in Wayne, NJ, so Paterson events are quoted from the same inventory as the rest of North Jersey. Tables and seating

Round tables come in 48″, 60″ and 72″ sizes, seating 6, 8–10 and 10–12 guests. Add 24″ or 30″ cocktail tables for standing areas, and choose Chiavari chairs in white, gold, black, silver, transparent, natural wood or dark wood, or folding chairs for larger headcounts.
