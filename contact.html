What to include in your request

The quickest quotes include your event date, the venue or city, your guest count and the items you want. If you built a quote list from the catalog, the items appear on the quote form so you can send them with your request.

Helpful details

Tell us whether the event is indoors or outdoors, the floor and elevator access, and your load-in window. For gallery walls, include the wall length and whether you want modular or stationary panels.
