Temporary walls for Chelsea shows

Chelsea is known for its galleries, and shows there often need extra hanging space for a pop-up, a fair booth or an opening without a permanent build. ZNC Solutions delivers 4′ × 7′ gallery wall panels from Wayne, NJ, in modular runs or as freestanding stationary walls.

Plan the wall with panel math

Each panel is 4 feet wide by 7 feet tall, so a 20-foot wall uses five panels and a 12-foot wall uses three. Send the wall length, ceiling height, door widths and load-in window and we confirm the panel count. A room divider covered in the fabric of your choice can screen off back-of-house areas.

Furnish the opening

Add seating from event furniture & lounge rentals, a bar with glassware, and table and chair rentals for a reception area. Coat racks, hangers and numbered coat check tickets are in the event equipment category.
