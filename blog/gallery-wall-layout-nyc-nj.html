How to Plan a Gallery Wall Layout for a Pop-Up or Opening in NYC and New Jersey

A temporary show lives or dies on wall space. Whether you are filling a loft in Brooklyn, a storefront in Jersey City or a hall in Bergen County, the planning steps are the same. Here is the order we suggest.

1. Count the works and group them

List every piece you plan to hang, then group them by series or size. Groups tell you how many separate bays or runs you need.

2. Do the panel math

Our wall panels are 4′ wide by 7′ tall. Each panel adds 4 feet of hanging width, so 5 panels make a 20-foot wall. Divide your total hanging width by 4 to get a panel count, then add a panel or two if you want breathing room at the ends of a run.

3. Pick the layout

Panels can run in a straight line, turn corners to make bays, or stand back to back for double-sided display. Modular runs connect panels into one continuous wall. Stationary panels stand alone and work well for short dividers. A double-sided run lets you hang two groups of work in the same footprint.

4. Check how the panels get in the room

In NYC and in older NJ buildings, access decides the plan: door widths, stairs, the freight elevator and the loading window. Send these details with your request so the delivery is planned around them.

5. Plan the rest of the room

Leave circulation space in front of every wall. Many openings add a bar, lounge seating and cocktail tables so guests have somewhere to stand and talk. See our wall panels, lounge furniture and event bars.

6. Send us the plan

Email info@zncsolutions.com with the date, venue, wall length or panel count and your layout idea. We confirm availability before anything is reserved. You can also sketch the wall with our online wall planner.
