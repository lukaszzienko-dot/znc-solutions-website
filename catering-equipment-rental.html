Catering equipment rental vs. kitchen equipment

This page covers the equipment guests see or that sits on the service line: chafers, buffet displays, serving pieces and coffee. If you are cooking on-site and need ovens, fryers, burners, proofing cabinets or refrigeration, see Kitchen Equipment Rentals. Many caterers book both on one quote.

Planning a buffet

Count the hot dishes on the menu to set the number of chafers, then add fuel for the length of service. Risers and trays help the cold side of a buffet read as full. Let us know if the venue restricts open flames, so we can plan electric hot holding instead.
