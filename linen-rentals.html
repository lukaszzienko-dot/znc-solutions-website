Tablecloths and napkins for your tables

Linens set the color of a room. ZNC Solutions quotes tablecloths and napkins from Wayne, NJ for parties, weddings and corporate events in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, with colors and textures chosen from our linen swatch library.

Match linens to your tables

Linens are ordered against your table plan. Our round tables come in 48″, 60″ and 72″ sizes, cocktail tables in 24″ and 30″, and there are rectangular and farmhouse tables as well. Tell us how many of each table type you are renting and we quote the linens to match.

Coordinate with chairs and tabletop

Pick a linen color alongside the chair finish: Chiavari chairs come in white, gold, black, silver, transparent, natural wood and dark wood. Then add china, glassware and flatware from tabletop and glassware. See table and chair rentals for tables and chairs.

Beyond tables

A custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric, with an optional logo, so a bar can match the linens. A room divider can also be covered in the fabric of your choice. See bars and event furniture & lounge rentals.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose linen colors?

Pick colors and textures from the linen swatch library and include them in your quote request.

Do you quote linens with tables?

Yes. Linens are quoted alongside the tables and chairs so everything is confirmed together.

Can a bar screen match my linens?

Yes. The custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric.
