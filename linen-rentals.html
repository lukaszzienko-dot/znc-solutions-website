Tablecloths listed by size and shape

Linens set the color of a room. Every tablecloth on this page is labeled with its full size and shape, so you can tell a 120″ round from a 90″ × 132″ rectangle or a square overlay. ZNC Solutions quotes tablecloths and napkins for parties, weddings and corporate events in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

Fabrics in the inventory include Signature, Rattan, Panama, Velvet, cotton and classic polyester. Tell us the colors you want, or send a photo, and we will confirm what is available for your date.

Match linens to your tables

Linens are ordered against your table plan. Our round tables come in 48″, 60″ and 72″ sizes, cocktail tables in 24″ and 30″, and there are rectangular and farmhouse tables as well. Tell us how many of each table type you are renting and we quote the linens to match.

Two cloths are already tied to a specific table in our records: the 90″ × 120″ rectangle, including the Panama version, is listed for a 4′ × 18″ × 36″ table, and the 102″ × 144″ rectangle is listed for a 6′ × 30″ × 36″ table. For every other size, send the table measurements and we will confirm the cloth.

Napkins

We rent napkins in these confirmed sizes: 18″ × 18″ hemstitched dinner napkins, 6″ × 6″ hemstitched cocktail napkins in packs of 30, and 4.5″ × 4.5″ linen cocktail napkins. Paper cocktail napkins come in packs of 50 and are listed as color choice. For cloth napkins, tell us the color you want and we will confirm what is in stock.

Coordinate with chairs and tabletop

Pick a linen color alongside the chair finish: Chiavari chairs come in white, gold, black, silver, transparent, natural wood and dark wood. Then add china, glassware and flatware from tabletop and glassware. See table and chair rentals for tables and chairs, or browse the full list on the linens category page.

Beyond tables

A custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric, with an optional logo, so a bar can match the linens. A room divider can also be covered in the fabric of your choice. See bars and event furniture & lounge rentals.

Frequently asked questions

What tablecloth sizes and shapes do you rent?

Round tablecloths in 84″, 90″, 96″, 108″, 114″, 120″ and 132″; square cloths and rattan overlays; and rectangular cloths including 90″ × 132″, 90″ × 156″ and other listed sizes. Each item is labeled with its full size and shape.

Do you rent napkins, and in which colors?

Yes. We rent 18″ × 18″ hemstitched dinner napkins, 6″ × 6″ hemstitched cocktail napkins in packs of 30, 4.5″ × 4.5″ linen cocktail napkins, and paper cocktail napkins in packs of 50 listed as color choice. For cloth napkins, tell us the color you want and we will confirm what is available.

How do I choose linen colors?

Tell us the colors and textures you want, or send a photo, and we will match from the inventory. Classic polyester 90″ × 156″ cloths and paper cocktail napkins are listed as color choice.

Do you quote linens with tables?

Yes. Linens are quoted alongside the tables and chairs so everything is confirmed together.

Can a bar screen match my linens?

Yes. The custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric.

How do I get a linen quote?

Call 917-536-1245, email info@zncsolutions.com, or use the quote form with your date, venue, table sizes and guest count.
