Where a room divider helps

Lofts, ballrooms, galleries and offices rarely come with a back room in the right place. A divider screens the caterer's prep and plating area from guests, hides cases, crates and empty boxes, closes off a coat check, or splits a large floor into a reception side and a dinner side. Because you pick the fabric, the divider can match the linens or the brand colors instead of looking like an afterthought.

FAQ

What is the room divider covered with?

You choose the fabric that covers the panel. Tell us the color or fabric when you request a quote.

What can I use a room divider for at an event?

Screening catering prep, storage or coat check from guests, making a green room, or splitting a large room into separate areas.

Room divider or wall panel: which do I need?

A fabric room divider is for screening space. For a solid surface to hang art or signage, use our double-sided 4x7 ft wall panels.

Do you deliver room dividers in New York City?

Yes. We deliver across NYC, NJ & CT. Include the venue and load-in window with your request.
