Event furniture and equipment for New York City

ZNC Solutions supplies event rentals for New York City work and public-facing gatherings: office holiday receptions, client dinners, product launches, gallery openings, press previews and brand activations. One catalog covers seating, tables, linens, bars, lounge furniture, tabletop, glassware, catering equipment and double-sided gallery wall panels, so a producer can gather the floor plan on a single quote list.

This page is written for people booking an event rather than a private party. If you are planning a birthday, wedding or apartment celebration, start with party rentals NYC and the borough pages linked below.

Tables, chairs and place settings

Seated dinners, award programs and conference lunches usually begin with table size. A 48″ round seats 6, a 60″ round seats 8–10 and a 72″ round seats 10–12. Cocktail tables in 24″ and 30″ diameters hold a standing reception or a registration cluster near the door. Pair them with Chiavari chairs, folding chairs or barstools from the table and chair rentals and chair rental pages, or browse all tables and chairs and barstools.

When the event includes a plated or buffet meal, add china, flatware and glassware from tabletop and glassware rentals and glassware rental NYC.

Bars for receptions and openings

A rented bar gives guests a clear place to gather at a launch, opening night or corporate reception, including rooms that have no built-in service counter. Choose a bar and barware from bar rental or the bars catalog, then add ice tubs, tools and glassware to match the drink menu. Tight Midtown conference rooms often do well with a single compact bar; a larger loft or hall can take a longer run with more pickup points.

Lounge furniture for waiting rooms and VIP corners

Lounge seating turns an empty foyer, a pre-function corridor or a quiet corner of a loft into a place where guests can talk. Sofas, a chaise, coffee tables and a rug from event furniture and lounge rentals or lounge furniture work well outside a keynote room, beside a registration desk or in a sponsor lounge. Tell us the floor plan and we will help you size the grouping to the space you actually have.

Linens for rooms that photograph

Hotel event floors, gallery white cubes and office conference centers each read differently on camera. Linen rentals cover tablecloths and overlays so the tabletops match the rest of the room. Share a color direction or a photo of the space with your quote request and we will pair linens with the tables and chairs on the same list.

Gallery walls for exhibits and brand moments

Temporary exhibition and brand environments use our modular or stationary wall panels, each double-sided, 4 feet wide and 7 feet tall. Five panels make a 20-foot run. See gallery wall panels, portable gallery walls, and the neighborhood pages for Chelsea, Manhattan and Brooklyn.

How NYC event load-ins usually work

City buildings set their own vendor rules. A Midtown office tower may require a booked freight cab and a service entrance; a Chelsea gallery may have a narrow elevator; a Brooklyn studio may load through a roll-up door from the street. When you request a quote, include the floor, the freight elevator or dock window, the earliest drop-off and the latest pickup the venue allows, and any curb or parking limits near the door. We schedule delivery and pickup around those notes. Delivery across NYC, NJ & CT.
