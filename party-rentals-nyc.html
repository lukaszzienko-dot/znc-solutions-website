Planning tips for NYC venues

City buildings and venues often have their own rules for vendors. Check these with your venue early and include them when you request a quote, so delivery and pickup can be planned around them:

  • Certificate of insurance (COI): ask the building or venue whether vendors need a COI and what names it must list.
  • Freight elevator or loading dock: ask for the booking window, size limits and whether deliveries must use the service entrance.
  • Load-in and load-out times: note the earliest drop-off and the latest pickup the venue allows.
  • Floor and access: the floor number, stairs, and any parking or curb restrictions near the entrance.
