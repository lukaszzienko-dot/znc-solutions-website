Party rentals for New York City events

ZNC Solutions delivers party and event rentals into Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens from Wayne, NJ. One catalog covers tables, chairs, linens, tabletop, glassware, bars, lounge furniture, kitchen equipment and 4′ × 7′ gallery wall panels, so planners can build a single quote list instead of sourcing from several vendors.

Furniture and tabletop

See Manhattan tables and chairs and Brooklyn tables and chairs for seating and tables, or browse event furniture & lounge rentals for sofas, a chaise, coffee tables and a rug. Round tables seat 6 (48″), 8–10 (60″) and 10–12 (72″), and cocktail tables come in 24″ and 30″.

Gallery walls for NYC shows

Galleries, museums and pop-ups use our modular or stationary 4′ × 7′ panels for temporary exhibition space. A 20-foot wall uses five panels. See gallery wall panels or the neighborhood pages for Chelsea, Manhattan and Brooklyn.

Planning an NYC delivery

Many city buildings have freight elevator schedules, loading rules and insurance requirements. Share those details, the floor and the load-in window with your quote request so delivery and pickup are planned around them. ZNC is based in Wayne, NJ; Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens are service areas.
