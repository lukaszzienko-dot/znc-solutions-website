Room divider, partition wall or temporary wall?

These rigid 4′ × 7′ panels give you a solid, freestanding partition wall without a permanent build. Use a short run to divide a gallery, loft or event space, or join panels into a longer temporary wall for a trade show, opening or photo wall. Because each panel is double-sided, both faces can work as an event wall or display surface. See also the main gallery wall panels page and portable gallery walls.

This page is about the rigid wall panels. ZNC also rents a separate fabric-covered room divider for screening off back-of-house space. Choose the solid panels when you need a partition wall or photo wall; choose the fabric divider when you need a fabric screen.

FAQ

Do you offer room divider rental in NYC?

Yes. We deliver rigid 4′ × 7′ partition walls and temporary walls across New York City, New Jersey and Connecticut. Add panels to your quote list or call 917-536-1245.

Are these pressurized apartment walls?

No. These are rigid, freestanding, double-sided 4′ × 7′ wall panels — solid event walls, not pressurized apartment walls.

Can I use the panels as a photo wall or for trade show walls?

Yes. The same panels work as a photo wall, event wall or trade show walls, and both sides can be used. Share your layout, panel count and venue with the quote request.

Is this the same as the fabric room divider?

No. This page covers the rigid 4′ × 7′ wall panels. The fabric-covered room divider is a separate rental for screening off a space. See the Room Divider page in Event Equipment if you need the fabric option.

How do I get a quote for a partition wall or temporary wall?

Add the wall panels to your quote list on this page and send it with your dates, venue and layout, or call 917-536-1245 or email info@zncsolutions.com.
