Warehouse and loft looks

Long farmhouse tables with X-back rustic chairs are the go-to for industrial Brooklyn venues. Dark wood or natural wood Chiavari chairs soften a concrete room, and black wooden barstools line a bar rail. We plan around loading docks, stairs and street parking you flag in the quote request.

Backyards and brownstones

For a backyard dinner, 48″ rounds seat six and slip through narrow side gates; folding chairs stack for quick changeovers between a ceremony and dinner. Add propane heaters, umbrellas, linens and glassware to keep the order in one delivery.
