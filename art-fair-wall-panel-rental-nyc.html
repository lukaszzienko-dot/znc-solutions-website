Why double-sided panels suit fair booths

Because both faces of every ZNC panel can take work, a single spine of panels down the middle of a booth or exhibition hall doubles the hanging surface. Five panels in a line make a 20-ft wall with 20 ft of hanging space on each side. Panels can also stand along the booth edges as back and side walls, or turn corners to make bays for smaller works.

Planning walls for an art fair booth

Start from the booth plan the fair sends you. Add up the length of every wall you need in feet and divide by 4 to get the panel count. A booth with a 12-ft back wall and two 8-ft side walls takes 7 panels; a 20-ft center spine takes 5 more and adds hanging space on both faces. Check the fair's rules on wall height against the 7-ft panel height.

When you request a quote, send the fair name and venue, booth number and size, the load-in and load-out windows, and any dock or freight elevator details. You can also sketch a layout with our online wall planner. For gallery shows and pop-ups outside a fair, see portable gallery walls, and for galleries in the neighborhood see Chelsea gallery walls.
