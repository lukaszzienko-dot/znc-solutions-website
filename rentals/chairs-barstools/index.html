Planning chairs & barstools for your event

Chiavari chairs are the usual choice for weddings and seated dinners, and ZNC carries white, gold, black, silver, transparent, natural wood and dark wood finishes. Folding chairs cover ceremonies and larger headcounts, and barstools pair with high-top and cocktail tables.

Count one chair per seat: a 60″ round seats 8–10 and a 72″ round seats 10–12. Send your guest count and table plan and we will confirm availability for your date.

To rent, add items to your quote list and send it with your date, city and guest count, or call 917-536-1245. ZNC delivers from Wayne, NJ across North Jersey, New York City and Connecticut.
