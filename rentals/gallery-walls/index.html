Planning gallery walls for your event

Gallery walls give galleries, pop-ups and brand activations temporary hanging space without a permanent build. Each panel is 4 feet wide by 7 feet tall, so a 20-foot wall uses five panels.

Choose modular panels for a long continuous run or stationary walls for a freestanding layout. A room divider covered in the fabric of your choice splits a space or screens off back-of-house areas.
