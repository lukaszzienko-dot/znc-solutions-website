Planning gallery walls for your event

Gallery walls give galleries, pop-ups and brand activations temporary hanging space without a permanent build. Each panel is a rigid, freestanding 4-foot-wide by 7-foot-tall event wall, so a 20-foot wall uses five panels. They also work as a temporary exhibition wall or photo wall. These are solid panel walls, not pressurized apartment walls.

Choose modular panels for a long continuous run or stationary walls for a freestanding layout. For a rigid room divider or partition wall in NYC, see Room Divider & Partition Wall Rental NYC. A separate fabric-covered room divider is also available if you need to split a space or screen off back-of-house areas.

To rent, add items to your quote list and send it with your date, city and guest count, or call 917-536-1245. ZNC delivers across North Jersey, New York City and Connecticut.
