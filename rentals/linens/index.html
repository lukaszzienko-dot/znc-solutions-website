Planning linens for your event

Each tablecloth in this category is listed with its full size and shape: round, rectangular or square, plus square rattan overlays. Dinner and cocktail napkins are listed with their sizes too. Fabrics in the inventory include Signature, Rattan, Panama, Velvet, cotton and classic polyester.

A custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric. Share your table plan and colors with the quote, and see linen rentals for the full size list. Paper cocktail napkins (packs of 50, color choice) are listed with barware.

To rent, add items to your quote list and send it with your date, city and guest count, or call 917-536-1245. ZNC delivers across North Jersey, New York City and Connecticut.
