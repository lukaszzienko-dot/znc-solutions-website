Planning tables for your event

ZNC rents 48″, 60″ and 72″ round tables that seat 6, 8–10 and 10–12 guests, 24″ and 30″ round cocktail tables for standing guests, plus farmhouse and rectangular tables for buffets, gift tables and family-style meals.

Share your guest count and whether the event is seated or standing and we will help plan the layout.
