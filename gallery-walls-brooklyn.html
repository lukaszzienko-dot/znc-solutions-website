Gallery walls for Brooklyn shows

Brooklyn is a ZNC service area; we are based in Wayne, NJ. From there we quote 4′ × 7′ gallery wall panels for pop-ups, open studios and exhibitions in Williamsburg, Bushwick, Downtown Brooklyn and other Brooklyn neighborhoods.

Panels, layouts and quantities

Panels are 4 feet wide and 7 feet tall and can be set up as a modular run or as stationary walls. A 20-foot wall is five panels. A room divider covered in your choice of fabric can split a space or hide back-of-house areas, and a custom fabric bar screen can be made in any tablecloth fabric with an optional logo.
