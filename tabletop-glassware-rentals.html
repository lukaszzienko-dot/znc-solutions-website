Tabletop for plated dinners and buffets

ZNC Solutions rents china, glassware, silverware, coffee service and serving pieces from Wayne, NJ for events in New Jersey, New York and Connecticut. Quantities follow your menu, your number of courses and your guest count.

China and serving pieces

China includes tasting plates and bowls in many sizes, round and square plates, and oval, angle and round platters. Serving pieces include trays, cake stands, risers, forks, spoons, tongs and ladles. Sizes are listed in each item name so you can plan the buffet.

Glassware and flatware

Glassware covers red and white wine, stemless wine, flutes, coupes, martini, old fashioned, highball and shot glasses, plus a glass pitcher. Flatware comes in Gold Silverware, Eclipse Flatware and Square Flatware. Capacity is listed on each glass.

Coffee and tea service

Coffee makers in 30-cup and 100-cup sizes, a French press, a samovar, tea pot, creamers, cups and saucers round out dessert service.
