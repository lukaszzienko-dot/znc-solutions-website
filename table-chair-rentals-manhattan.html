Pick the right table size

A 48″ round seats 6 and fits most apartment living rooms; 60″ rounds seat 8–10 and are the standard for dinners; 72″ rounds seat 10–12 when you need fewer tables in a ballroom. Rectangular tables handle buffets and registration, and 24″ or 30″ cocktail tables keep a standing reception moving.

Chairs for every room

Chiavari chairs come in white, black, gold, transparent, natural wood and dark wood. White and black folding chairs cover ceremonies and presentations, while X-back, wooden and Chiavari barstools pair with cocktail tables. Add linens, china and glassware to the same order.
