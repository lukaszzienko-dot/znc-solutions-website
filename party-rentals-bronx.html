Party rentals for Bronx halls, homes and waterfront rooms

The Bronx mixes settings that each ask for a slightly different rental list. Riverdale apartments and private homes host dinners and milestone parties in club rooms or living rooms. Fordham and University Heights add campus-adjacent receptions and student or faculty gatherings. Arthur Avenue restaurants and social clubs run seated dinners. City Island leans toward waterfront get-togethers. Mott Haven and Port Morris have lofts and studios. Community halls across the borough handle receptions and cultural celebrations. ZNC Solutions delivers tables, chairs, linens, bars, lounge furniture and gallery walls to all of them.

Tables and chairs for halls, club rooms and houses

A community hall or social club is usually a 60″-round floor, sometimes with a head table on rectangular banquet tables. Club rooms in Riverdale apartment buildings are smaller and may only fit 48″ rounds or a single long table. A private house can go either way, depending on whether the meal stays indoors or moves to a backyard or driveway tent the host already has. Chiavari chairs dress a seated dinner; folding chairs are a solid match for halls and outdoor overflows onto a patio. See table and chair rentals, chair rental, all tables and chairs and barstools. Cocktail tables help in a lobby or at a hall entrance during a receiving line.

Bars for receptions, clubs and waterfront gatherings

Some Bronx halls already have a built-in bar; many club rooms and waterfront rentals do not. A station from bar rental or bars can sit at the short end of a hall, on a club-room wall, or on a City Island patio if the surface is level and covered. Add glassware when the toast is more than a disposable cup service. If the venue limits where drinks can be poured, put that rule in the quote request so the bar is planned for the approved spot.

Lounge seating for lobbies and club rooms

Apartment-building club rooms and hall lobbies often have a television and a few house chairs, but not enough soft seating when a family arrives early or when speeches run long. A sofa, a loveseat and a coffee table from event furniture and lounge rentals or lounge furniture fill that gap without turning the room into a dining floor. In a loft in Mott Haven or Port Morris, the same grouping can anchor a conversation area away from the tables.

Linens for hall dinners and at-home milestones

Hall receptions look finished when every round wears the same cloth; a house dinner may only need a few linens that match a family color or a seasonal palette. Linen rentals are quoted with the tables so the sizes line up. If a hall provides some cloths and not others, send a count and we will complete the set rather than replace it.

Gallery walls for campuses and community exhibits

Campus shows, community-center exhibits and loft pop-ups in the South Bronx need walls that can be delivered, used and taken away without altering the building. Each panel is double-sided and measures 4 feet wide by 7 feet tall, which is a comfortable height for hanging work and for a title or sponsor panel on the reverse. See gallery wall panels and portable gallery walls. Five panels make a 20-foot wall, a useful length along a hallway exhibition or a studio's long side.

Highways, halls, houses and park gates

Many Bronx venues are easier to reach by highway than a Midtown tower, but the last hundred feet still decide the plan. A hall may have a back door or a short driveway and a clear schedule for vendors. A Riverdale high-rise needs elevator hours. A walk-up or a private house may load from the curb or a driveway. Park-adjacent events at Van Cortlandt Park, Bronx Park or Pelham Bay Park often depend on a vehicle gate, a paved path or a field that will not take a loaded dolly after rain — send whatever the permit or park staff has told you. City Island's tighter streets are worth a note about where a truck can stand. Include the entrance you want us to use, and the earliest and latest times the venue allows.

Party rentals in the Bronx: frequently asked questions

Do you deliver party rentals to the Bronx?

Yes. ZNC Solutions delivers to Bronx neighborhoods including Riverdale, Fordham, Arthur Avenue, Mott Haven and City Island, with pickup after the event.

Can you deliver to a community hall or a private house?

Yes. Halls often have a back door or a short driveway. Private houses and Riverdale apartment buildings vary between elevators, walk-ups and a curb drop. Share the entrance you want us to use.

What is typical for a Bronx hall reception?

A field of 60″ rounds with chairs and linens, plus a bar if the hall does not have a fixed one. Lounge seating can fill a lobby or a quiet side room.

Do you bring gallery walls to the Bronx?

Yes. Double-sided panels, 4 feet by 7 feet, are quoted for campus shows, community exhibitions and loft pop-ups in places such as Mott Haven and Port Morris.
